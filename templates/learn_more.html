About WasteCare

A smart digital platform for cleaner communities, responsible waste reporting and better waste management.

🌱 What is WasteCare?

WasteCare is a digital waste management platform designed to connect citizens with a structured waste reporting and monitoring system.

Users can report waste or illegal dumping locations, attach supporting images, provide location details and track the progress of their submitted reports.

🎯 Our Objective

The main objective of WasteCare is to make waste reporting easier, faster and more transparent.

📍 Report waste locations
📸 Upload supporting images
🗺️ Identify waste locations
📋 Track submitted reports
🔔 Receive notifications
♻️ Encourage responsible disposal

⚙️ How WasteCare Works

1

Report

A citizen identifies a waste or dumping problem and submits a report.

2

Locate

The location information helps identify where the waste problem exists.

3

Monitor

Submitted reports can be reviewed and monitored through the system.

4

Resolve

Authorities or responsible teams can process the reported waste issue.

🗑️ Major Types of Waste

🥬

Organic Waste

Food scraps, garden waste and other biodegradable materials.

♻️

Recyclable Waste

Materials such as paper, cardboard, glass and suitable plastics.

🔌

E-Waste

Discarded electronic and electrical equipment that requires proper handling.

🧴

Plastic Waste

Plastic packaging and other discarded plastic materials.

♻️ Why Waste Segregation Matters

Waste should be separated at the source whenever possible. Keeping different waste streams separate makes collection, recycling, composting and appropriate treatment easier.

🥬 Wet / Organic Food and biodegradable waste
📦 Dry / Recyclable Paper, cardboard, glass and suitable plastics
🔌 Special Waste E-waste and materials requiring special handling

🌍 Global Waste Challenge

2.56B tonnes of municipal waste generated globally in 2022
3.86B tonnes could be generated annually by 2050 under business-as-usual
♻️ Waste prevention, reuse, recycling and better collection can reduce the pressure on waste systems.

Facts based on recent World Bank and UNEP assessments of global municipal solid waste.

👥 What Citizens Can Do

  • ♻️ Separate waste before disposal.
  • 🛍️ Reduce unnecessary single-use items.
  • 🔄 Reuse products whenever practical.
  • 📦 Prefer recyclable and reusable materials.
  • 📍 Report illegal dumping through WasteCare.
  • 🌱 Support cleaner neighbourhoods and communities.

💡 Why WasteCare?

Traditional waste complaints can be difficult to track when information is scattered or not recorded systematically.

WasteCare provides a centralized digital workflow where reports, locations, images, statuses and notifications can be managed through one platform.
